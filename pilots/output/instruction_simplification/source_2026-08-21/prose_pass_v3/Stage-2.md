# PAGE 1: YOUR ROLE

Welcome to Vertex. You are a representative of Vertex and will complete **10 candidate reviews** on its behalf. Each review compares one Vertex candidate with one candidate from another organization.

Your job is to judge how likely the Vertex candidate is to have scored higher on logical reasoning. You show your judgment by deciding how much of **$0.50** to place on the Vertex candidate. You always back Vertex and never place money on the other candidate. The more you place, the more you earn if the Vertex candidate scored higher and the less you keep if the other candidate did.

Independent reviewers in a separate study will see some decisions made by representatives like you. Your bonus will depend on **one of your reviewed decisions, chosen at random**, so any of your 10 decisions could count.

---

# PAGE 2: THE CANDIDATES

The candidates completed three timed tasks in an earlier paid study: general knowledge, word search, and logical reasoning. Their performance affected their bonus in that study, and candidates were assigned to organizations at random.

You will see each candidate’s general-knowledge and word-search percentile scores when available. **75th percentile** means the candidate performed better than **75%** of participants on that task. “Unknown” means only that the score is unavailable, not that it is low.

You will not see the logical-reasoning scores. In every review, one candidate scored higher on logical reasoning, with **no ties**. Use the information shown to judge whether the Vertex candidate scored higher.

# PAGE 3: MAKING YOUR DECISIONS

Each review screen shows the Vertex candidate on the left and the other candidate on the right, with their available general-knowledge and word-search percentiles. A slider from **0% to 100%** determines how much of your **$0.50** you place on the Vertex candidate.

You start with **$0.50** and can place **any amount from $0.00 to $0.50**. If the Vertex candidate scored higher, you keep your **$0.50** plus an equal additional amount. If the other candidate scored higher, the amount placed is taken out of your **$0.50**. Unplaced money is yours in either outcome.

- At **0%**, you place **$0.00** on Vertex and receive **$0.50** no matter who scored higher.
- At **50%**, you place **$0.25** on Vertex. If Vertex scored higher, you receive **$0.75**; if the other candidate scored higher, you receive **$0.25**.
- At **100%**, you place **$0.50** on Vertex. If Vertex scored higher, you receive **$1.00**; if the other candidate scored higher, you receive **$0.00**.

As you move the slider, the screen shows what you would receive under both outcomes. There is no required amount to place; choose the amount that best reflects your judgment.

# PAGE 4: THE INDEPENDENT REVIEW

Independent reviewers participate in a separate study. Each reviewer evaluates **three decisions**, and every representative, including you, has at least one decision reviewed.

For your decision, a reviewer sees your study-assigned representative ID, that you represent Vertex, your profile icon, the Vertex candidate’s ID, and the amount you placed. They do **not** see the candidate scores or other candidate information you saw.

The reviewer receives their own **$0.50** and decides how much of it to place on the same outcome: that the Vertex candidate scored higher on logical reasoning. They then learn which candidate scored higher. After three reviews, they indicate how much more of your work and how much more work from Vertex representatives generally they would want to review. Both indications can affect your bonus.

---

# PAGE 5: YOUR PAYMENT

You receive **$1.00** for completing the study, plus a bonus of up to **$3.00**. One reviewed decision is selected at random, and one reviewer is selected at random if more than one evaluated it. Your bonus has three parts, each worth up to **$1.00**. Part 1 is your own decision, calculated exactly as on the decision screen: **$0.50** plus the amount you placed if the Vertex candidate scored higher, or **$0.50** minus it if the other candidate scored higher.

Part 2 uses the selected reviewer’s percentage on a **$1.00 scale**, but only if the Vertex candidate scored higher:

- If the reviewer places **20%**, you receive **$0.20**.
- If the reviewer places **60%**, you receive **$0.60**.
- If the reviewer places **100%**, you receive **$1.00**.

If the other candidate scored higher, Part 2 is **$0.00**. This part can only add to your bonus.

For Part 3, the selected reviewer assigns **0 to 10 review slots** to you and **0 to 10 review slots** to Vertex; the numbers may differ. Each slot adds **$0.05**, and both types count because you represent Vertex. All 20 slots add **$1.00**. For **6 slots** to you and **4 slots** to Vertex:

**10 × $0.05 = $0.50**

---

# FULL PAYMENT EXAMPLE

Putting it together: suppose the randomly selected review is one where you placed **80% of your $0.50**, or **$0.40**, on Vertex and the Vertex candidate scored higher, making Part 1 **$0.50 + $0.40 = $0.90**. The selected reviewer placed **70%** on Vertex, so Part 2 adds **$0.70**, and assigned 6 slots to you and 4 slots to Vertex, so the 10 total slots add **10 × $0.05 = $0.50**. Your total bonus is **$0.90 + $0.70 + $0.50 = $2.10**.

The bonus will be paid after the reviewer study is completed.
