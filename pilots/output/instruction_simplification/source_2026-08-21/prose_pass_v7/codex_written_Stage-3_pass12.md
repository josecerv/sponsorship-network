# PAGE 1: YOUR ROLE

You will review decisions made by another participant, called the representative, who took part in an earlier study on behalf of [Atlas/Vertex]. Each decision compared one [Atlas/Vertex] candidate with one candidate from another organization. The representative decided how much of **$0.50** to place on the [Atlas/Vertex] candidate having scored higher on logical reasoning. The representative could only back the [Atlas/Vertex] candidate, so the decision was how much to place. They could see the candidates’ scores on two other tasks, but not their logical-reasoning scores.

For each decision, you will see the representative, the [Atlas/Vertex] candidate’s ID, and the amount the representative placed. You will not see the candidates’ scores.

---

# PAGE 2: MAKING YOUR REVIEWS

For each review, you receive a new **$0.50**. A slider from **0% to 100%** sets how much of it you place on the [Atlas/Vertex] candidate. If the [Atlas/Vertex] candidate scored higher, you receive **$0.50** plus the amount you placed; if the other candidate scored higher, you receive **$0.50** minus the amount you placed. The screen shows what you would receive under both outcomes. Place the amount that best reflects your judgment.

After each review, you learn which candidate scored higher and what you earned. You keep what you earn on every review. These earnings are paid as a bonus on top of your **[$X.XX]** participation payment.

Your decisions, and your answers to two short questions at the end, may also contribute to the representative’s bonus. Your own earnings always come from your reviews.
