# PAGE 1: YOUR ROLE

You will review three decisions made by another participant, called the representative. The representative took part in an earlier study and was assigned to Atlas. Each decision compares a different pair: one Atlas candidate and one candidate from another organization.

For each review, you receive **$0.50** to place on the same Atlas candidate. You then learn which candidate scored higher and how much you earned. You keep the earnings from all three reviews, up to **$3.00**.

Your decisions may also contribute to the representative’s bonus through a random selection explained later. Your own earnings always come from your three reviews.

---

# PAGE 2: WHAT THE REPRESENTATIVE KNEW

The candidates completed three timed tasks in an earlier paid study: general knowledge, word search, and logical reasoning. Their performance affected their bonus, and candidates were assigned to organizations at random. For each pair, one candidate scored higher on logical reasoning.

The representative saw each candidate’s general-knowledge and word-search percentiles when available, but not their logical-reasoning scores. A percentile compared the candidate’s performance with other participants in that study. “Unknown” meant that a score was unavailable. They used the visible information to judge whether the Atlas candidate scored higher on logical reasoning.

For each decision, the representative decided how much of **$0.50** to place on the Atlas candidate; they could only place money on the candidate from their own organization. If the Atlas candidate scored higher, they received **$0.50** plus the amount they placed; if the other candidate scored higher, they received **$0.50** minus the amount they placed.

Placing more backed the Atlas candidate more strongly with the representative’s potential earnings. The representative knew that other participants would later see some decisions.

---

# PAGE 3: MAKING YOUR REVIEWS

You will see the three decisions in an order that may differ from the order in which they were made. For each, you see the study-assigned representative ID, that they represented Atlas, their profile icon, the Atlas candidate’s ID, and the amount placed. You do **not** see either candidate’s general-knowledge, word-search, or logical-reasoning score.

You start each review with a new **$0.50**, which works the same way as the representative’s, and a slider from **0% to 100%** sets how much of it you place on the Atlas candidate.

- At **0%**, you place **$0.00** on Atlas and receive **$0.50** no matter who scored higher.
- At **50%**, you place **$0.25** on Atlas. If Atlas scored higher, you receive **$0.75**; if the other candidate scored higher, you receive **$0.25**.
- At **100%**, you place the full **$0.50** on Atlas. If Atlas scored higher, you receive **$1.00**; if the other candidate scored higher, you receive **$0.00**.

Place the amount that best reflects your judgment. The screen shows what you would receive under both outcomes. After each review, you learn which candidate scored higher on logical reasoning and your earnings. You keep all three amounts; there is **no random selection** for these earnings. If they are **$0.75**, **$0.20**, and **$0.50**, you receive:

**$0.75 + $0.20 + $0.50 = $1.45**

---

# PAGE 4: HOW YOUR DECISIONS CAN AFFECT THE REPRESENTATIVE

For the representative’s bonus, the study selects one reviewed decision and one reviewer at random. If you are selected, two parts of your decisions can add to the bonus.

Part 1 is the amount you placed, converted to a **$1.00 scale** if the Atlas candidate scored higher:

- If you placed **20%**, the representative receives **$0.20**.
- If you placed **60%**, the representative receives **$0.60**.
- If you placed **100%**, the representative receives **$1.00**.

If the other candidate scored higher, Part 1 is **$0.00**. This differs from your own **$0.50 review budget**.

Part 2 uses review slots decided at the end: how much more of the representative’s work and how much more work from Atlas representatives generally you would want to review. If you are selected, each slot adds **$0.05** regardless of the outcome, up to **$1.00**. Both parts can add up to **$2.00** combined. They affect only the representative’s bonus; your own earnings are always the total from your three reviews.
