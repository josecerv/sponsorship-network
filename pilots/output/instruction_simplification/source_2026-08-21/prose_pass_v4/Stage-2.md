# PAGE 1: YOUR ROLE

Welcome to Vertex. You are a representative of Vertex and will complete several candidate reviews on its behalf. Each review compares one Vertex candidate with one candidate from another organization.

Your job is to judge how likely the Vertex candidate is to have scored higher on logical reasoning. You show your judgment by deciding how much of **$0.50** to place on the Vertex candidate. You always back Vertex and never place money on the other candidate; the only decision is how much, so place the amount that best reflects your judgment.

Later, independent reviewers in a separate study will see some of your decisions, including your representative profile (a study-assigned ID, that you represent Vertex, and a profile icon), the Vertex candidate’s ID, and the amount you placed, but not the candidates’ scores, and will make their own decisions about them. Your bonus will depend on **one of your reviewed decisions, chosen at random**, so any of your decisions could count.

# PAGE 3: MAKING YOUR DECISIONS

Each review screen shows the Vertex candidate on the left and the other candidate on the right, with their available general-knowledge and word-search percentiles. Below them, a slider from **0% to 100%** sets how much of your **$0.50** you place on the Vertex candidate. If the Vertex candidate scored higher, you receive **$0.50** plus the amount you placed; if the other candidate scored higher, you receive **$0.50** minus the amount you placed.

- At **0%**, you place **$0.00** on Vertex and receive **$0.50** no matter who scored higher.
- At **50%**, you place **$0.25** on Vertex. If Vertex scored higher, you receive **$0.75**; if the other candidate scored higher, you receive **$0.25**.
- At **100%**, you place **$0.50** on Vertex. If Vertex scored higher, you receive **$1.00**; if the other candidate scored higher, you receive **$0.00**.

As you move the slider, the screen shows what you would receive under both outcomes.

# PAGE 4: YOUR PAYMENT

You receive **$1.00** for completing the study, plus a bonus of up to **$3.00**. One reviewed decision is selected at random, and one reviewer is selected at random if more than one evaluated it. Your bonus has three parts, each worth up to **$1.00**. Part 1 is your own decision, calculated exactly as on the decision screen: **$0.50** plus the amount you placed if the Vertex candidate scored higher, or **$0.50** minus it if the other candidate scored higher.

Part 2 comes from the selected reviewer, who placed their own **$0.50** on the same outcome. It uses the percentage the reviewer placed, on a **$1.00 scale**, but only if the Vertex candidate scored higher:

- If the reviewer places **20%**, you receive **$0.20**.
- If the reviewer places **60%**, you receive **$0.60**.
- If the reviewer places **100%**, you receive **$1.00**.

If the other candidate scored higher, Part 2 is **$0.00**. This part can only add to your bonus.

For Part 3, the selected reviewer indicates how much more of your work, and how much more work from Vertex representatives generally, they would want to review, by assigning **0 to 10 review slots** to you and **0 to 10 review slots** to Vertex; the numbers may differ. Each slot adds **$0.05**, and both types count because you represent Vertex. All 20 slots add **$1.00**. For **6 slots** to you and **4 slots** to Vertex:

**10 × $0.05 = $0.50**

---

# FULL PAYMENT EXAMPLE

Putting it together: suppose the randomly selected review is one where you placed **80% of your $0.50**, or **$0.40**, on Vertex and the Vertex candidate scored higher, making Part 1 **$0.50 + $0.40 = $0.90**. The selected reviewer placed **70%** on Vertex, so Part 2 adds **$0.70**, and assigned 6 slots to you and 4 slots to Vertex, so the 10 total slots add **10 × $0.05 = $0.50**. Your total bonus is **$0.90 + $0.70 + $0.50 = $2.10**.

The bonus will be paid after the reviewer study is completed.
