## VARIANT B

Welcome aboard [Vertex/Atlas], your candidate organization in this study. Real people who completed three timed tasks in an earlier study are its candidates, put forward for independent review. [Vertex/Atlas] seeks a strong showing against candidates from other organizations and depends on you to speak for them.

As its representative, you speak for [Vertex/Atlas] candidates. Each review compares a [Vertex/Atlas] candidate with one from another organization. You choose how much of **$0.50** to place behind the [Vertex/Atlas] candidate. You always back [Vertex/Atlas]; you decide only the amount, so choose what best reflects your judgment.

In a separate study, independent reviewers will see some of your decisions. They decide how far to follow each with their own money and whether they want more of your decisions. Those decisions shape their view of you and [Vertex/Atlas]. Your bonus depends on **one of your reviewed decisions, picked at random**, so any decision could count.
