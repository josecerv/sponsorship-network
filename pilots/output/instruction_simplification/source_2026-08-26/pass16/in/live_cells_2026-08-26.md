## STAGE 2|2|0

Your role

Welcome to [Vertex/Atlas]. You are a representative of [Vertex/Atlas] and will complete several candidate reviews on its behalf. Each review compares one [Vertex/Atlas] candidate with one candidate from another organization.

Your job is to judge how likely the [Vertex/Atlas] candidate is to have scored higher on logical reasoning. You show your judgment by deciding how much of $0.50 to place on the [Vertex/Atlas] candidate. You always back [Vertex/Atlas] and never place money on the other candidate; the only decision is how much, so place the amount that best reflects your judgment.

Later, independent reviewers in a separate study will see some of your decisions. Your bonus will depend on one of your reviewed decisions, chosen at random, so any of your decisions could count.


---
## STAGE 2|5|0

Making your decisions

Each review screen shows the [Vertex/Atlas] candidate on the left and the other candidate on the right, with their available general-knowledge and word-search percentiles. Below them, a slider from 0% to 100% sets how much of your $0.50 you place on the [Vertex/Atlas] candidate. If the [Vertex/Atlas] candidate scored higher, you receive $0.50 plus the amount you placed; if the other candidate scored higher, you receive $0.50 minus the amount you placed.

At 0%, you place $0.00 on [Vertex/Atlas] and receive $0.50 no matter who scored higher.
At 50%, you place $0.25 on [Vertex/Atlas]. If [Vertex/Atlas] scored higher, you receive $0.75; if the other candidate scored higher, you receive $0.25.
At 100%, you place $0.50 on [Vertex/Atlas]. If [Vertex/Atlas] scored higher, you receive $1.00; if the other candidate scored higher, you receive $0.00.

As you move the slider, the screen shows what you would receive under both outcomes.

## STAGE 2|6|0

Your payment

You get $1.00 for finishing the study. You can also get a bonus of up to $3.00.

Your bonus is based on one of your reviews, picked at random. The bonus has two sources. The first is your own decision: $0.50 plus the amount you placed if the [Vertex/Atlas] candidate scored higher, or $0.50 minus it if the other candidate scored higher. The second part comes from the independent reviewer: how much of their own money they were willing to place behind the candidate you endorsed, and how much more of your work they wanted to review afterward. Together, these decisions determine up to $2.00 of your bonus.

You get the bonus after the reviewer study is finished.

Your role

You will review decisions made in an earlier study by another participant, called the representative, on behalf of [Atlas/Vertex].
