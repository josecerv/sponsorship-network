# PAGE 1: YOUR ROLE

This study is set up like a staffing agency with two organizations, Vertex and Atlas. You are an independent reviewer and do not belong to either organization.

You will review referrals made in an earlier study by another participant, called the representative, on behalf of [Atlas/Vertex]. Each decision paired an [Atlas/Vertex] candidate with a [Vertex/Atlas] candidate. The representative referred the [Atlas/Vertex] candidate and decided how much of **$0.50** to place on that candidate having scored higher on logical reasoning. The representative knew that independent reviewers would see some of the referrals, and the more they placed, the more of their own pay rode on that candidate. They could see the candidates’ scores on two other tasks, when available, but not their logical-reasoning scores.

For each decision, you will see the representative’s information, the [Atlas/Vertex] candidate’s ID, and the amount they placed. With the **$0.50** you receive for each review, your job is to decide how far to follow the representative’s referral.

---

# PAGE 2: MAKING YOUR REVIEWS

For each review, you receive a new **$0.50**. A slider from **0% to 100%** sets how much of it you place on the [Atlas/Vertex] candidate. If the [Atlas/Vertex] candidate scored higher, you receive **$0.50** plus the amount you placed; if the other candidate scored higher, you receive **$0.50** minus the amount you placed. The screen shows what you would receive under both outcomes. Place the amount that best reflects your judgment.

After each review, you learn which candidate scored higher and what you earned. You keep what you earn on every review. These earnings are paid as a bonus on top of your **[$X.XX]** participation payment.

After your reviews with this representative, you will make one choice about your final review, explained when you reach it, and after that review you will answer one question about this representative. Your placements and your answer may also add to the representative’s bonus; the choice does not. Your own earnings always come from your reviews and from that final choice.

# PAGE 6B: CHOOSE YOUR FINAL REPRESENTATIVE

For your final review, use the slider to choose how likely it is that the decision comes from this same representative rather than a different representative drawn at random from the other representatives. The slider runs from **0%**, certainly a different representative, to **100%**, certainly this representative, in **10-point** steps.

You receive **$0.15** for this choice and keep whatever the choice does not cost. At **50%**, a coin flip decides and the choice costs **$0.00**, so you keep the full **$0.15**. The further you move from **50%**, the more each step costs:

- **50%**: **$0.00**
- **60%** or **40%**: **$0.01**
- **70%** or **30%**: **$0.03**
- **80%** or **20%**: **$0.06**
- **90%** or **10%**: **$0.10**
- **100%** or **0%**: **$0.15**

After you choose, one random draw uses your chosen chance to select the representative for your final review. This choice affects which representative your final review comes from and how much of the **$0.15** you keep. It does not affect the representative’s bonus.

**C.** Nothing changes unless you move the slider to **100%**.
