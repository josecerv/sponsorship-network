# PAGE 1: YOUR ROLE

Welcome to [Vertex/Atlas]. This study is set up like a staffing agency with two organizations, Vertex and Atlas, and you have joined [Vertex/Atlas] as a representative. Your decisions will be seen: in a separate study, independent reviewers will see some of your referrals. For each one, a reviewer decides how much of their own money to place behind the candidate you backed, and at the end chooses how likely it is that one more review comes from you rather than another representative, and can spend money to shift that chance. Your decisions shape the view they form of you and of [Vertex/Atlas].

Each review compares one [Vertex/Atlas] candidate with one [Atlas/Vertex] candidate on logical reasoning. You always refer the [Vertex/Atlas] candidate; the only decision is how much of **$0.50** to place behind them, so place the amount that best reflects your judgment.

Your bonus depends on **one of your reviewed decisions, picked at random**, so any decision could count.

# PAGE 3: MAKING YOUR DECISIONS

The amount you place shows how strongly you vouch for the [Vertex/Atlas] candidate. Independent reviewers will see it as your confidence in the referral. The more you place, the more of your own pay rides on the candidate, which makes the signal credible.

Each review screen shows the [Vertex/Atlas] candidate on the left and the other candidate on the right, with their available general-knowledge and word-search percentiles. Below them, a slider from **0% to 100%** sets how much of your **$0.50** you place on the [Vertex/Atlas] candidate. If the [Vertex/Atlas] candidate scored higher, you receive **$0.50** plus the amount you placed; if the other candidate scored higher, you receive **$0.50** minus the amount you placed.

- At **0%**, you place **$0.00** on [Vertex/Atlas] and receive **$0.50** no matter who scored higher.
- At **50%**, you place **$0.25** on [Vertex/Atlas]. If [Vertex/Atlas] scored higher, you receive **$0.75**; if the other candidate scored higher, you receive **$0.25**.
- At **100%**, you place **$0.50** on [Vertex/Atlas]. If [Vertex/Atlas] scored higher, you receive **$1.00**; if the other candidate scored higher, you receive **$0.00**.

Being right pays you directly, in proportion to how much you placed, and makes reviewers more willing to follow your next referral and to choose you again.

As you move the slider, the screen shows what you would receive under both outcomes.

# PAGE 4: YOUR PAYMENT

You get **$1.00** for finishing the study. You can also get a bonus of up to **$3.00**.

Your bonus is based on one of your reviews, picked at random. The bonus has two sources. The first is your own decision: **$0.50** plus the amount you placed if the [Vertex/Atlas] candidate scored higher, or **$0.50** minus it if the other candidate scored higher. The second part comes from the independent reviewer: how much of their own money they were willing to place behind the candidate you referred, and how much more of your work they said they would want to review. Together, these decisions determine up to **$2.00** of your bonus.

You get the bonus after the reviewer study is finished.
