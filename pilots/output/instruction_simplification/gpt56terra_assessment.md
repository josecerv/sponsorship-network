## Bottom line

**Not safe to field exactly as written.** The proposal is mostly shorter without changing the fixed design, and the main payout components remain present. However, several edits create avoidable ambiguity in the payment rules or drop an explicit fact from the original. Fix the **must-fix** items below before fielding.

The most important risks are:

1. **V-R8:** “$0.50 + your wager” does not clearly distinguish a selected percentage from the dollar amount wagered.
2. **V-R7:** “If yours is picked” has an unclear antecedent and blurs the conditional random-selection rule for the endorser’s pay.
3. **E-R8:** “Unknown = no score” changes the stated meaning. The original says the participant has no data for that candidate/task, not that the candidate lacks a score.
4. **E-R6:** The allocation maximum should be explicitly tied to the combined 20 possible slots, and “win/lose” should be tied back to logical-reasoning performance on the earnings screen.
5. **E-R5:** The original’s disclosure that allocation happens at the end of the evaluator’s session has been dropped.
6. **V-R6:** Use the required term **“endorsement strength”** when describing the information evaluators see, rather than only “confidence.”

## Incentive and payment audit

### Endorser, E-R6

The proposed version preserves the intended three-part bonus structure:

1. **Candidate-performance component:** $0.50 plus or minus $0.50 times endorsement strength.
2. **Evaluator-trust component:** conditional on candidate win, $1.00 times evaluator wager percentage.
3. **Allocation component:** $0.05 per slot, with 20 possible slots and a $1.00 total maximum.

The stated **$3 maximum** remains correct: $1.00 candidate-performance maximum + $1.00 evaluator-wager maximum + $1.00 allocation maximum.

The issue is not that the math is wrong. The issue is that a skimmer can miss that the allocation maximum is **combined across the two 0–10 allocations**, and can encounter “win” without its outcome definition on the payout screen.

### Evaluator, V-R8

The proposed version preserves the correct endpoint values:

- 0% wager: $0.50 regardless of correctness.
- 100% wager: $1.00 if correct, $0.00 if wrong.
- Three decisions: up to $3.00 total.

But the formula needs to distinguish the **percentage selected** from the **dollar amount wagered**. The existing 10% example partly repairs this, but it should not be the only place that makes the conversion explicit.

### Random-selection rule

- **E-R6** is broadly adequate: one endorsement and one evaluator who reviewed it are selected at random.
- **V-R7** is not adequately clear as written because “If yours is picked” does not say what “yours” means or make clear that this rule governs the **endorser’s** bonus. This needs repair before fielding.

## Cuts that went too far

1. **E-R8, “Unknown.”** This is the only clear substantive wording change. “No score” is not the same as “you do not have data.” Restore the original meaning.
2. **E-R5, allocation timing.** “At the end of their session” was explicitly disclosed in the original and should remain.
3. **V-R6, endorsement-strength terminology.** The proposal turns the displayed variable into generic “confidence.” Retain “endorsement strength,” optionally followed by “their stated confidence.”
4. **V-R7, random-selection scope.** The original conditional rule survives in intent but not with sufficient clarity in the proposed wording.

The proposal does **not** improperly cut the following fixed facts: own-organization-only endorsements, three evaluator rounds, independent candidate sets, evaluators’ lack of candidate performance data, immediate feedback after each wager, and the organization-comparison rule.

---

## Remaining bloat worth cutting

The main remaining bloat is **V-R5**. The study-purpose sentence is legacy framing, not instruction. Replace it with an action-oriented bridge or reduce it to one sentence.

A smaller cut is the opening sentence of **V-R6**, “Before you wager, here is the setup.” The labels “Candidates,” “Endorsers,” and “Your task” already provide structure; the lead-in can be removed without loss.

Do **not** cut:

- the 10% wager example in V-R8;
- the 0% and 100% endpoint examples in V-R8;
- the repeated must-endorse reminder in E-R10;
- the allocation conditionality in V-R7/V-R14.

Those are short, high-value protections against common skim errors.

## Tone

There is no meaningful condescension. The proposal is mostly direct and participant-facing. The only residual filler is the generic research-purpose framing in V-R5 and, to a lesser degree, “Before you wager, here is the setup” in V-R6. The shorthand style, such as “Higher = a stronger endorsement,” is acceptable for Prolific participants and improves scanning.

## Overall verdict

**Do not field as written.** Field after the **must-fix edits to E-R5, E-R6, E-R8, V-R6, V-R7, and V-R8.** After those fixes, the simplified version should be safe to field: it retains the fixed design, preserves the three-part endorser bonus and $3 maximum, keeps the evaluator’s three-round $0.50-bank payment rule, and leaves all comprehension checks answerable from the instructions alone.
