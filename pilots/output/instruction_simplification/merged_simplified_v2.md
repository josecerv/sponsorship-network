## ENDORSER (Stage 2)

### E-R4 — intro + task + slider  (was ~200 words, now ~118)
You are an endorser for [ORGANIZATION 1: Vertex].
Each candidate set has two candidates: one from [ORGANIZATION 1: Vertex] (your organization's candidate) and one from [ORGANIZATION 2: Beacon]. You will see the available quiz and word-search performance information for both candidates.
Your task is to set your endorsement strength for your organization's candidate. You are not choosing between the two candidates: you always endorse the [ORGANIZATION 1: Vertex] candidate. Base your strength on how likely your candidate is to score higher than the other organization's candidate in the same set on a separate logical reasoning test.
The slider runs 0 to 100. Higher = a stronger endorsement: more confident, more behind your candidate. Lower = a weaker endorsement: less confident, less at risk.
Evaluators will see your endorsement as [ORGANIZATION 1: Vertex] backing its own candidate.

### E-R5 — who sees your endorsements  (was ~95, now ~58)
Evaluators (participants in a separate study) review your endorsements. For each one, they see: your organization, the candidate you endorsed (your organization's), and your endorsement strength.
They then decide what percent of a $0.50 bank (0-100%) to wager on your endorsement being correct. At the end of their session, they decide how much future review to allocate to you and to your organization.

### E-R6 — earnings  (was ~250, now ~140)
Earnings: $1 for completing the study, plus a bonus of up to $3.
One of your endorsements is picked at random, along with one evaluator who reviewed it. Your bonus for that endorsement has three parts:
1. Candidate performance. Start with a $0.50 bank. Your endorsement strength works like a wager. Win means your candidate scores higher on the logical reasoning test than the other organization's candidate in that set; lose means your candidate scores lower.
   Win: $0.50 + ($0.50 x your strength percentage)
   Lose: $0.50 - ($0.50 x your strength percentage)
   At strength 80: $0.90 if your candidate wins, $0.10 if your candidate loses.
2. Evaluator trust. If your candidate wins, add $1.00 x the evaluator's wager percentage.
3. Evaluator allocations. The evaluator allocates 0-10 future review slots to you and 0-10 to your organization. Each slot = $0.05, up to $1.00 total, paid win or lose.

### V-R6 — setup  (was ~230, now ~132)
Before you wager, here is the setup.
Candidates: earlier participants who did three tasks: a word-search puzzle, a general-knowledge quiz, and a logical-reasoning test.
Endorsers: separate participants, each assigned to represent an organization. Each decision showed an endorser two candidates (their own organization's and another organization's), plus only the quiz and word-search scores. The endorser used a slider to show how confident they were that their own organization's candidate would score higher than the other organization's candidate in the same set on the logical-reasoning test.
Your task: judge how much to trust one endorser. You will see a subset of their real decisions (order may vary), each showing their organization, the candidate endorsed (always their own organization's), and their confidence, but not the candidates' performance data. You then wager part of your pay on whether the endorser was right.

### V-R7 — endorser incentives telegraph  (was ~140, now ~110)
Endorsers had real incentives to endorse carefully: their pay works like a wager. A stronger endorsement earns more if their candidate scores higher on the logical-reasoning test than the other organization's candidate in that set, and earns less if their candidate scores lower.
Their pay also depends on you:
- If their candidate wins: the more you wager, the bigger their bonus.
- At the end, you allocate review slots to this endorser and their organization; each slot adds to their bonus, win or lose.
For this endorser's bonus, one endorsement is selected at random, along with one evaluator who reviewed it. If your session is selected, your wager on that endorsement and your allocations directly affect this endorser's pay.

### V-R8 — wager mechanics + rounds  (was ~170, now ~120)
You will evaluate three decisions from the same endorser, each a new, independent candidate set.
Each decision: start with a $0.50 bank and choose a wager percentage (0-100%). Your wager amount is that percentage of $0.50.
- Right: $0.50 + the amount you wagered
- Wrong: $0.50 - the amount you wagered
Example, 10% wager ($0.05): right = $0.55, wrong = $0.45.
0% = keep your $0.50 regardless. 100% = $1.00 or $0.00.
Correct means the endorsed candidate scored higher on the logical-reasoning test than the other organization's candidate in the same set. Your bonus from these three decisions = the sum of all three (up to $3.00). You will see the result right after each wager.
